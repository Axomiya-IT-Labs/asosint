# ASOSINT

> Verify Before You Trust.

ASOSINT is an open-source Digital Trust platform focused on OSINT, cybersecurity awareness, AI literacy, digital investigations, online privacy, and verification.

It is NOT a hacking website.

It is NOT a penetration testing platform.

It is NOT a cyber attack toolkit.

Its mission is to educate people, provide safe verification tools, and help everyone navigate the digital world safely in the age of Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Mission

Help everyone answer one question:

"Can I trust this?"

Whether it is

- an image
- a video
- a voice recording
- an email
- a QR code
- a PDF
- a social media account
- a website
- a news article
- an AI generated conversation

ASOSINT teaches people how to verify before trusting.

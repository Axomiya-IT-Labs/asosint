# ASOSINT Roadmap

# Vision

Build the world's most trusted open-source Digital Trust platform.

ASOSINT aims to become the go-to resource for:

- OSINT
- Cybersecurity Awareness
- AI Literacy
- AI Verification
- Online Privacy
- Scam Prevention
- Digital Investigations

Our mission is simple:

We build for the long term.

Quality always comes before quantity.
